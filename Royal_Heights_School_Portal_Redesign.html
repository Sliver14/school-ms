Curricular Distinction

An Uncompromising Standard of Learning

Our dual-curriculum model integrates international critical inquiry with strong Nigerian educational heritage.

Junior Secondary (JSS 1 – 3)

Laying solid foundations in mathematical logic, language arts, basic sciences, computer coding, and French bilingual proficiency.

  • National Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE)
  • Cambridge Lower Secondary Checkpoint Testing
  • Weekly Creative Problem Solving & STEM Labs

Senior Secondary (SS 1 – 3)

Specialized Science, Commercial, and Humanities divisions preparing candidates for premier tertiary admissions across Nigeria and worldwide.

  • WAEC (SSCE) & NECO Senior School Certification
  • Cambridge IGCSE International Examination stream
  • SAT, IELTS & University Admissions Guidance

STEM Innovation & AI Robotics

State-of-the-art computational suites equipping learners with Python, 3D prototyping, mechatronics, and artificial intelligence basics.

  • Modern Physics, Chemistry & Biology Laboratories
  • First Lego League & Robotics Championships
  • Full-stack Web & Mobile Coding Apprenticeship
Simple & Transparent

Enrolment for 2026/2027

Join an ambitious community of students dedicated to intellectual mastery and moral integrity.

01

Online Application

Submit candidate particulars, prior academic transcripts, and personal statement via our digital admissions desk.

02

Entrance Assessment

Candidate sits for scholastic aptitude evaluations in Mathematics, English Comprehension, and General Reasoning.

03

Family Consultation

An interview with the Vice Principal and Head of Pastoral Care to align values, goals, and boarding preferences.

04

Offer & Enrolment

Successful candidates receive formal letters of admission and access to the student onboarding portal.

Ready to Take the First Step?

Applications for JSS1, JSS2, SS1, and SS2 entrance exams are processed on a rolling basis. Limited places available per stream.
